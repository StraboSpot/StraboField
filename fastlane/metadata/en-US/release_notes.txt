SAMPLES & IGSN
• IGSNs from strabospot.org: IGSNs now come from your strabospot.org account, so the app no longer signs in to or registers with SESAR
• IGSNs on strabospot.org: View IGSN and Get IGSN open the sample's own page on strabospot.org
• Upload first: a sample not yet on your account offers to upload the project, then opens its page when the upload is done
• IGSN edits reminder: editing a sample that has an IGSN reminds you to upload so strabospot.org can update it
• Correct sample location: a sample from an image-basemap Spot gets a real-world location

FORMS & VALIDATION
• Live form validation: errors show as you type and Save stays disabled until they're fixed
• Find the problem field: the tab holding an unanswered field is marked so you know where to look
• Auto dip direction: entering a strike fills in the matching dip direction wherever you type one
• Negative numbers on iOS: type negative values, with a keyboard matched to the field
• Sensible limits: number fields reject impossible values, like negative thicknesses, percentages over 100, or azimuths past 360
• Number pad on iOS: fields that can't be negative open the number pad instead of the full keyboard

COMPASS
• Automatic declination: magnetic declination is recorded and kept up to date in your project data
• Declination without GPS: falls back through your project location and nearby Spots to stay corrected, with clearer warnings when it can't
• Lineation hint: "Align long side with lineation" shows for linear measurements

LOCATION
• Location button spinner: the Geography location button shows a spinner while it waits for a fix
• Location checked first: shortcuts and geolocate check that location is on and allowed before they start, and tell you if it isn't
• Clear location errors: a failed location lookup says why, and shortcuts no longer leave the spinner running
• Location Services off: a separate message tells you to turn Location Services back on in Settings
• Coordinate checks: Geography coordinates must be numbers, so a typo can no longer leave a Spot without a location

MEASUREMENTS
• Bulk strike/dip: calculate missing strikes and dip directions for every record that holds a plane
• Steadier validation: planar+linear and 3D-structure measurements are checked and hinted more reliably

TAGS
• Memos on tags: add, remove, count, and list a tag's memos from its detail page
• Cleaner tag counts: a tag's counts show as icon chips instead of a legend
• Sample tagging: only samples that have their own Spot can be tagged
• Synced tags stay current: saving a tag after an auto-sync no longer overwrites edits made on another device

MEMOS
• Memo authorship: memos record who wrote them and when
• Associated samples: link samples to a memo, including legacy samples, from the Memo modal
• Memos in Overview: a Spot's memos now show in its notebook Overview

NOTEBOOK & PAGES
• Feature labels: every feature list shows a label, filled in on save
• Full Spots list: see the full Spots list when no Spot is selected
• Collapsible More Pages: the More Pages sections collapse like the main menu's
• Tidier Overview: Sedimentary Rocks is hidden from the Overview when no lithology has rock data

LISTS & DATASETS
• Remembered sort: each list keeps its sort order and reverse sort, applied before the first paint
• Active dataset names: lists name the active datasets at the top, with a shortcut to open Datasets
• Map filter for Spots: filter Spots by whether they're on the geographic map or not mapped
• Edited Spots stay put: editing a Spot on the map no longer also adds it to the target dataset, so it can't vanish from its own dataset after an upload
• Fix Spots in two datasets: on the Metadata page, tap a dataset to move a Spot listed in several datasets into just that one

Full notes: https://github.com/StraboSpot/StraboField/releases/tag/v2.32.0

Thanks for using StraboSpot!
