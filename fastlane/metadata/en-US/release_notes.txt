StraboField 2.31.0 is a big update — richer Samples with full IGSN support, much of the app now working on the web, and improvements across maps, the notebook, sketching, and sync.

SAMPLES & IGSN
• Richer Samples: hold images, measurements, and more — just like Spots — with a gold border and banner, visible in nesting, and taggable
• Add Sample screen: attach images and assign geologic units right when you create a sample
• Get an IGSN: register and manage IGSNs end-to-end, with a Get IGSN button and an upload progress bar
• Keep IGSNs in sync: prompts to update SESAR when relevant fields change, with a View IGSN Data link and offline warnings

NOW ON THE WEB
• Freehand drawing: draw freehand lines and polygons in the web app
• Stereonet lasso: lasso-select measurements on the stereonet
• Export & import: back up and import tags, geologic units, and templates

DRAWING & EDITING
• Freehand vertex spacing: control how closely points are placed when you draw freehand lines and polygons
• Extend a line: drag out a new endpoint to make an existing line longer
• Overlapping Spot picker: when several Spots sit under your tap, choose exactly which one to select
• Undo Spot delete: deleted a Spot by mistake? Tap the undo toast to bring it back (not on web)

MAP DISPLAY & SYMBOLS
• Dike symbol: a new map symbol for the dike planar feature type
• Symbol labels: label map symbols with Dip/Plunge/Name
• UTM coordinates: toggle a UTM coordinate display on the map
• Colored strat intervals: strat section intervals take on their tag or geologic-unit colors

NOTEBOOK & FORMS
• Outcrop Summaries: a new notebook page with one summary per Spot
• More Pages menu: reorganized into five clearer sections
• Surface-feature detail: added quality and notes fields

PHOTOS & SKETCHING
• Save sketches your way: save a sketch over an image as a copy or an update, with a heads-up before you overwrite
• Zoom & pan while sketching: pinch to zoom and drag to pan when drawing on an image

LISTS & SEARCH
• Powerful search & filters: a shared search bar with grouped multi-select filters for Spots and Tags
• Live map extent lists: your lists update automatically as you move the map — no button to press
• Inspect Raw Data: expanded by default, and now available on web

COMPASS & MEASUREMENTS
• Compass: redesigned dial with animated headings and tick marks
• Measurement mode that sticks: your manual vs. compass preference now syncs and is remembered

BACKUP, SYNC & OFFLINE
• Auto-save: saves your work automatically alongside manual backup, with adjustable frequencies and countdown timers
• Backup status: a status screen and status-bar icons show what's pending, with a Save Now button
• Offline profile sync: your profile syncs even after you've been offline

ACCOUNTS & SIGN-IN
• Forgot Password: reset your password right from the sign-in screen
• Session handling: prompts you to sign back in when your session expires instead of failing silently

SMALL TOUCHES
• Battery status: battery icons now show charging vs. discharging
• Conventions remembered: unsaved convention changes are kept and toggles stay in sync
• Checkbox selection: multi-select now uses checkboxes instead of a full-row highlight
