PHOTOS
• Taking photos on iPad: taking photos, especially several in a row, no longer crashes the app

MEASUREMENTS
• Steady compass for lineations: trend and plunge are always taken along the top of the screen, which now stays in portrait while the compass is open, instead of sometimes reading 90° or 180° off depending on how the device was held. The live strike/dip and trend/plunge are shown under the compass

NOTEBOOK & NAVIGATION
• Zoom to Spot: zooming to a Spot opened from the Spot Navigator no longer crashes or reopens the navigator
• Notebook tab on phones: the Notebook tab no longer goes blank after opening a project
• Notebook from the image viewer: the Notebook button opens the Notebook instead of a second map

PHOTOS & BACKUPS
• Photo resize errors: a photo that fails to resize shows an error instead of closing the app
• Re-importing backups: images already on the device are copied correctly when a backup is imported again

MAPS & SIGN-IN
• Downloaded custom maps: a custom map without an id no longer stops a downloaded project's custom maps from loading
• Forgot password: when the reset link can't be opened, the sign-in screen tells you where to reset it
