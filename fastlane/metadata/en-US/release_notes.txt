FORMS & MEASUREMENTS
• Live validation: errors show as you type, Save stays disabled until they're fixed, and the tab with the problem field is marked
• Strike and dip direction fill each other in
• Negative numbers can be typed on iOS
• A fresh compass reading takes priority over an active template
• Every feature has a Label, with a default filled in if you leave it blank

COMPASS
• Without GPS, declination falls back to your project location or nearby Spots, with clearer warnings when it can't

TAGS & MEMOS
• Add, remove, and count a tag's memos from its detail page
• Memos show who wrote them and when
• Link samples to a memo
• A Spot's memos show in its Overview

LISTS & DATASETS
• Lists remember their sort order
• Lists show the active datasets, with a shortcut to Datasets
• Filter Spots by whether they're on the map

READ-ONLY DATASETS
• Spots can't be moved, copied, or created into a read-only dataset
• Sample and Other Feature editing is locked on read-only Spots

SAMPLES & IGSN
• IGSN registration works with SESAR's new system
• You're prompted to sign in to SESAR when saving a sample with an IGSN

SHORTCUTS & PHOTOS
• Shortcuts open the new Spot in the Notebook
• Canceling a photo or sketch no longer leaves an empty Spot
• Photos picked together no longer overwrite each other

MAPS & STRAT SECTIONS
• Spots set on an image basemap or strat section stay on that map
• A Spot with several measurements shows its name label once
• Image overlays for strat sections
